# Assets and Imagery

## Asset language

Craft-inspired assets are a reusable visual language, not decoration for its own sake.

Approved families:

- Loom-line patterns
- Thread and weave motifs
- Simple hand-drawn craft icons
- Brass, textile, wood, and clay material cues
- Abstract regional pattern fragments
- QR seal or signed-listing mark
- Audio waveform illustrations for artisan workflows only
- Small artisan-process illustrations

## Product imagery rules

- The actual product image is the hero of the public catalog.
- Use consistent crops and enough breathing room for product form and texture.
- Keep backgrounds quiet and material-appropriate.
- Never place a decorative pattern over the important part of a product image.
- Never generate or invent product photography, artisan identity, provenance, or regional claims.
- Do not use generic stock photos as substitutes for catalog products.
- Do not use the six internal studio background presets as decorative catalog backgrounds.
- Product media must come from the listing record or an explicitly approved asset.

## Decorative asset rules

- Use one dominant craft motif per section at most.
- Keep motifs near edges, separators, transitions, or explanatory diagrams.
- Do not use patterns behind small text or verification details.
- Motifs must have sufficient contrast and must not create visual noise.
- Hand-drawn assets should share stroke weight, corner character, and scale.
- The logo remains a user-supplied asset; do not invent a replacement mark.

## Demo asset rule

For early rendering, use one or two synthetic product records labelled `Mock — for SIH demo`. Replace them with database media later without changing the component structure.
