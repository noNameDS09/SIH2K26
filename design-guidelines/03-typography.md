# Typography

Typography must feel refined while remaining easy to read for users with low literacy, small screens, and multiple Indic scripts.

## Roles

- **Functional face:** a highly legible multilingual sans-serif for navigation, controls, filters, forms, verification, prices, and artisan workflow copy.
- **Editorial face:** a restrained expressive serif or display face for public headings, collection moments, and short product-story accents.
- **Fallback face:** a script-complete fallback must be defined for every supported language.

The final font pairing is not locked until it is tested in Hindi, Marathi, English, Tamil, Bengali, and Kannada. Latin-only display fonts are not acceptable for translated headings.

## Rules

- Never use all caps for Indic copy.
- Use sentence case for controls and instructions.
- Keep instructions short; split complex ideas into separate lines or steps.
- Do not use thin weights for body copy, metadata, or verification details.
- Keep body text comfortable at mobile size and allow translated strings to wrap.
- Use tabular or clearly aligned numerals for prices and measurements.
- Do not bake text into product images, illustrations, or decorative assets.
- Maintain a visible hierarchy between product name, maker, material, region, verification, and supporting details.
- Avoid decorative type effects, stretched lettering, excessive tracking, and text over busy imagery.

## Content length protection

Components must tolerate at least 30–50% expansion over the English copy without clipping, forced truncation, or broken buttons. Test long product names and translated filter labels before approval.
