Typography:
• Use Plus Jakarta Sans for interface text.
• Use Playfair Display for editorial headlines and heritage storytelling.
• Use a bold italic serif treatment for emphasized craft names.
• Use appropriate Devanagari-compatible typography for Hindi copy.
• Metadata and labels: 10–11px, medium/bold, often uppercase with 0.5–0.6px tracking.
• Body copy: 12px with approximately 18px line height.
• CTAs: 13px semibold.
• Card titles: 16px semibold.
• App-bar titles: 18px semibold.
• Editorial headings: approximately 28px with 35–36px line height and slightly negative tracking.

Components and styling:
• Maintain 20px horizontal mobile gutters and a 350px content column.
• Cards use 8px, 12px, or 16px corner radii.
• Buttons and status pills use fully rounded corners.
• Use restrained shadows such as 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.05), with occasional 0 2px 4px, 0 4px 6px, or 0 10px 15px depth.
• Headers and bottom navigation use 90% opaque ivory, 24px background blur, and a subtle brown-tinted shadow.
• Camera and AI diagnostic chips use dark translucent fills with background blur.
• Primary CTAs use terracotta backgrounds and white text.
• Use small line icons consistently for back, profile, audio, camera, upload, microphone, verification, download, arrows, marketplace, location, and navigation actions.
• Preserve the airy parchment-like page background and tightly organized card stack.
