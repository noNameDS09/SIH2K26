# 14 — FAQ (for agents)

## Why Flutter and a website?

PS title says mobile application. App is the hand demo. Web is the same listings plus `/market` so a judge can see publish land without installing the APK.

## Why FastAPI if Firestore exists?

OTP, **quality** image enhance, **web** Sarvam/Gemini, HMAC/QR, Admin trend job. Firestore is the document store, not the place to hide vendor keys for the browser.

## Why can the app call Gemini directly?

Latency on hold-to-talk. Keys gitignored. Demo-only risk. Web must not copy this.

## Is this a marketplace?

**App: no.** **Web `/market`: show catalog only.** No cart, no pay. Listings are projections of the artisan’s published docs.

## Why not Bhashini?

No short-notice access. Sarvam + Gemini. Do not add Bhashini “just in case.”

## Why not Postgres?

Varying craft fields. Firestore maps. Do not add SQL “because we used to.”

## What languages?

All Sarvam-supported Indic + English in the picker. Rehearse at least Hindi plus one South and one East if time is short, but **do not hide langs from the UI**.

## Can I skip studio quality to move faster?

No. A jagged cut-out fails the PS. Stay on `09` until the gate looks judge-ready.

## Can the advisor chat?

No. One sentence or silence.

## Can I train YOLO on our crafts first?

Not blocking. Server ISNet/BiRefNet is the quality path. Custom train is later.

## OTP real SMS?

Optional 2Factor. Default mock `123456` with badge. Do not block the app on DLT.

## Aadhaar?

Mock + badge. Always.

## GeM live?

No. Shaped JSON + mocked label.

## Where do I put new screens?

App: `lib/ui` with an ID from `04`. Web: `src/app/` route from `04` plus `src/components`. If the screen is a new **job**, update `04` first in the same PR.

## Why Next.js not Vite?

Human lock. App Router already lives in `apps/web`. Do not add Vite. Do not copy Flutter UI.

## What if `asia-south1` is unavailable?

Firestore: Stop. Tell the human. Do not pick US.

Storage: Spark/no-cost default bucket has no Mumbai option. Locked exception: **`US-EAST1`** (`kalasetu-903c2.firebasestorage.app`). That does not move Firestore.

## Can I use the mockup pixels?

No. Names and jobs only. Visuals are the client team’s.

## DemoSIH-main?

Read-only dummy. Never the product.

## How do I know the two clients are linked?

App publish → web `/market` shows it, and the reverse. That is the acceptance test in `12`.

## Provenance missing on a price?

Bug. Do not ship that screen.

## User said something that contradicts `agent-coding-guide`?

This turn’s user message wins **only if they are changing a lock**. Then update `agent-coding-guide` in the same change. Do not silently follow old pitch docs.
